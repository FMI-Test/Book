# The Driver

I had many drivers across different phases of life. Some came through family, some through circumstance, and some through formal hiring.

After years of relying on other people to manage movement and logistics, one lesson became clear: if the mission matters, you must own the standards.

This chapter starts a practical profile of the driver role I require: disciplined, precise, and calm under pressure.

## Driver Wanted

### Requirements

1. Special operations level defensive driving or equivalent tactical driving certification.
2. Verified clean legal record and identity background check.
3. Professional communication, confidentiality, and chain-of-command discipline.
4. Strong route planning and contingency awareness.
5. Demonstrated decision quality under stress.

### Evaluation Baseline

- Maneuverability and control
- Situational awareness
- Time and route discipline
- Professional behavior
